Our story

Built on honest work and word of mouth

Elias Crew Co. started with a simple idea: treat every home like it's our own, and the work will speak for itself. We're a family-owned business based right here in McKinney, led by Daniel, and we've grown the way a good contractor should — one happy neighbor telling the next.

When you hire us, you get Daniel and a dedicated crew — not a rotating cast of subcontractors you've never met. From a quick handyman repair to a full kitchen remodel, you deal with the same people from the first quote to the final walkthrough. We show up on time, give you an itemized estimate with no surprises, keep the job site clean, and back every job with a full 1-year workmanship warranty.
